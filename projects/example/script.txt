# Example story: "The Golden Banana" (11 shots, roughly 45 seconds)
# Format: SCENE lines say what we see, NAME: lines are spoken dialogue.
SCENE: Wide shot of a sunny jungle clearing at sunrise. Bobo and Mimi sit side by side on a tree branch.
Bobo: What a beautiful morning!
Mimi: And I am very hungry.
SCENE: Close-up of Bobo's face as he sniffs the air and smiles.
Bobo: I smell bananas!
SCENE: Bobo and Mimi swing from vine to vine through the trees, leaves flying past.
SCENE: The two monkeys stop at a tall banana tree with one golden banana at the very top.
Mimi: Only one banana?
SCENE: Bobo tries to climb the trunk, slips, and lands on his back in the grass.
Bobo: Ouch!
SCENE: Mimi laughs, then points up at the golden banana.
Mimi: Let's do it together.
SCENE: Mimi stands on Bobo's shoulders and stretches her arm toward the banana.
Mimi: Almost there...
SCENE: Mimi grabs the golden banana and holds it high in triumph.
Mimi: I got it!
SCENE: Mimi breaks the banana in half and hands one half to Bobo.
Mimi: Half for you.
SCENE: Bobo and Mimi sit side by side eating bananas with the sun shining behind them.
Bobo: Best friends share everything.
SCENE: Wide shot of the jungle in golden light, Bobo and Mimi small on a branch.
